# Sources and third-party material

This repository archives first-party research notes, benchmark harnesses and measurements, with attributed source metadata and sanitized historical evidence.

Cached upstream engine repositories, their Git history, and paper PDFs/TeX are retained only in the ignored local archive. Their original licenses and notices are preserved there. Public metadata records canonical URLs, fixed commits or paper versions, and original SHA-256 values so sources can be recovered.

GitHub/API observations, model metadata and external reports are attributed in the research assets and leaderboard protocol. External author measurements remain author reports; they are not claimed as reproduced measurements.

Model weights are not distributed by this repository. Follow the original model repositories and licenses when downloading them. No blanket license in this archive overrides the rights or terms of an upstream work.
